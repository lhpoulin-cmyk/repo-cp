# Repository governance ownership

repo-cp owns inventory, enrollment records, accepted contract pins, repository
audit implementation, drift reports and narrow remediation proposals. Foundation
owns universal contract vocabulary, invariants and conformance. Domain
repositories own declarations and desired state; ansible-cp realizes authorized
state. Louis decides enrollment, exceptions, ownership, migrations and merges.

repo-cp owns no inventoried target facet, credential, grant, host, VM, network or
service. It cannot promote a consumer, provider or observer to authority. The
target-facet contract is unresolved; no ownership assignment or virtualization-cp
is created. B70 remains NOT_ADMITTED; its observed 192.168.10.91 endpoint is not
desired network state; live mutation is NONE.

The local enrollment schema describes audit scope only, not a competing universal
Foundation standard. Actions are declarations for review; no executor exists.
